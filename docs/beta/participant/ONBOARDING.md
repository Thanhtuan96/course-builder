# Onboarding

The one page a participant reads before Day 0.

## What the beta is

A private beta with 3–5 builders, run over four weeks on macOS. Each participant uses Professor on
one real project of their own and tells us what actually changed in that project.

## What you need

- macOS 13 or newer.
- Node 22.5 or newer, because Studio stores its local data through `node:sqlite`.
- One web project you own and are allowed to change.
- A terminal you are comfortable running commands in.

## What is asked of you

Four check-ins, on Day 0, Day 3–4, Day 7 and Day 14. Each is a short call with three questions about
what you shipped, what got in your way, and what Studio produced that was better than your usual
setup. On Day 7 and Day 14 you also export a beta report and send the file.

## How long it takes

About 20 minutes per check-in, plus the time you already spend on your own project. Nothing extra is
scheduled between check-ins.

## What you get

Early access to the beta build, a direct line to the person building it, and a written summary of
what your reports showed at the end of the four weeks.

## First steps

1. Read the [screener](SCREENER.md) and confirm every knockout passes.
2. Confirm you meet the requirements above.
3. Read the [troubleshooting guide](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) so the first launch is not a surprise.
4. Reply with your participant id once it is issued.
5. Read the [consent note](CONSENT.md) and reply yes.
