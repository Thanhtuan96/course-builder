# Check-in script

## How to use

Record what happened, never what they liked. Write answers down verbatim: the wording is the
evidence, and a paraphrase turns a report into an opinion. Ask the three questions in the order
given, then stop talking and let the answer run.

## Call schedule

| Check-in | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Before the first session | Confirm setup, agree on the project, capture the baseline |
| Day 3–4 | Three to four days in | See what was actually attempted while the first friction is fresh |
| Day 7 | One week in | Capture week-1 evidence and the first report export |
| Day 14 | Two weeks in | Capture week-2 return, the capstone state and the final export |

## Day 0

Goal: the participant is set up, has chosen the project they will work in, and has said out loud what
they want to finish.

1. What did you actually ship or change in your own project since we last spoke?
2. Walk me through the last time you got stuck — what did you do next?
3. What did Studio produce that was better than Claude + your notes + your editor?

On Day 0 the first question is a baseline: they may have shipped nothing yet, and that is a valid
answer worth writing down exactly as said.

## Day 3–4

Goal: catch the first real friction and the first real attempt, before memory smooths it over.

1. What did you actually ship or change in your own project since we last spoke?
2. Walk me through the last time you got stuck — what did you do next?
3. What did Studio produce that was better than Claude + your notes + your editor?

## Day 7

Goal: close week 1 with both a conversation and the first machine-readable record.

1. What did you actually ship or change in your own project since we last spoke?
2. Walk me through the last time you got stuck — what did you do next?
3. What did Studio produce that was better than Claude + your notes + your editor?

Then: Ask them to export from Studio: Settings → Export beta report, and save it as
professor-beta-report-<participantId>-d<day>.json. This is the export we aggregate.

## Day 14

Goal: close the cohort with the week-2 evidence, the capstone state and the second export.

1. What did you actually ship or change in your own project since we last spoke?
2. Walk me through the last time you got stuck — what did you do next?
3. What did Studio produce that was better than Claude + your notes + your editor?

Then: Ask them to export from Studio: Settings → Export beta report, and save it as
professor-beta-report-<participantId>-d<day>.json. This is the export we aggregate.
