## Edge Coverage and Assumptions

PW-01 was `unclassified` in the deterministic probe (no SPEC.md). Its edges are resolved from the
ROADMAP success criterion and CONTEXT, and stated here rather than left implicit:

- A1 (empty): a template with no learner input still produces a complete course — seeding takes only
  a language. Covered by truth 1.
- A2 (adjacency): starting the template from New Course must not collide with the free-text/AI flow —
  the template card is a separate, additive surface above the existing form, and the existing
  "Skip AI" path is untouched. Covered by task 3 acceptance criteria.
- A3 (ordering): lessons keep their declared order (positions 1–5) and rubric criteria keep R1–R7
  order; both are asserted, not assumed.
- A4 (encoding): the Vietnamese variant is selected through `normalizeLanguage`, so legacy display
  names (`'Vietnamese'`) select it too. Covered by task 1 acceptance criteria.
- PW-02 stays model-judged for R1/R7, but the rubric **definitions** are fixed data here — truth 3.

## Reversibility gates

- **Gate A (after Task 1):** the content modules are pure data; nothing is persisted yet. Reverting is
  free. Do not proceed until the structural parity assertions between the two language variants pass.
- **Gate B (before Task 2 persists anything):** confirm seeding writes through `createCourse` and
  `createCapstone` only — no `updateCourse`, no `updateLesson`, no capstone update path. **This is the
  one-way step (D-01/D-02):** once a cohort starts, lesson text and rubric become learner-visible
  commitments.
- **Gate C (after Task 2):** running the seeder twice must leave the row counts unchanged. If the
  second call creates a second capstone, stop — `createCapstone`'s early return is the immutability
  guarantee, and a workaround would be a contract break.
